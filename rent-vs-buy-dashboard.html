This model leaves out the mortgage interest deduction, taxes on investment gains, mortgage insurance for down payments under 20 percent, and the chance that you move or refinance. Home appreciation and market returns are assumptions, not forecasts. The renter starts with the portfolio you set, or the down payment plus closing costs if you leave that on automatic, and invests the fixed monthly amount you choose, or the owning cost minus rent if you turn on the automatic option. When owning cost is below rent, the buyer invests the difference. This is a planning tool, not financial advice.
